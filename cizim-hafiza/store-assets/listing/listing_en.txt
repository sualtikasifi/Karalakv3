# Kısa açıklama
Draw it, then remember it! Daily challenge, monthly league, play with friends.

# Tam açıklama
In Karalak you draw first, then try to remember what you drew. Drawing is the easy half. Remembering is where the fun begins!

🎨 HOW TO PLAY
1. See the word and draw it before the timer runs out.
2. Take a short break and catch your breath.
3. Your own drawings come back: what was this one? Type the word and collect your points!

Being misunderstood is an artist's fate. Draw anyway!

🔥 DAILY CHALLENGE
Everyone gets the same words every day. Play daily and your streak grows; the longer the streak, the more XP each round pays. Miss a day and it resets, but you get a chance to make it up.

⚡ QUICK MATCH
One tap pairs you with an opponent at your level. Same words, same clock, same conditions. Your opponent may be another player's recorded round or an AI. Who remembered better is settled at the final whistle.

👫 PLAY WITH FRIENDS
Create a room, share the code and start together. Up to eight players, or 2v2 in teams. React with emojis mid-match and ask for a rematch when it ends. You can also send a friend a private duel.

🗺️ LEVELS
90 levels across 9 worlds on a map that keeps getting harder. Earn up to three stars on each.

🏆 MONTHLY LEAGUE
A monthly XP ranking against your friends and the world. Players who finish in the top three earn in-game rewards. (AI filler players take part in the league too; prizes go to real players only.)

✨ GROW AND CUSTOMIZE
• Level up and unlock avatar frames that change as you climb
• Colorful pen skins to draw in your own style
• Chests, jokers and Runes: unlock bonuses as you play
• 101 achievements, from easy to brutal

📴 WORKS OFFLINE
In Offline Play, pick 10 to 50 words and draw on your own. You only need a connection for online modes and cloud backup.

🌍 OVER A THOUSAND WORDS
Animals, objects, jobs, sports, nature, food, vehicles, emotions and clothing: over a thousand words in 9 categories. Answer checking is forgiving about accents and small typos. Available in English, Turkish, Spanish and Portuguese.

🔒 YOUR PROGRESS STAYS YOURS
Sign in with Google and your level, streak, achievements and level progress are backed up, so you can pick up where you left off on a new phone. You can delete your account and data from inside the app at any time.

Grab your pen. Remember. Compete. Karalak is waiting!
